## Overview

**Independent surf-and-city week in San Sebastián, Spain — no camp, just a board rental and a lesson or two.** This is the best fit for the "we can already surf, we don't need a camp" brief: base in one of the world's great food cities (pintxos, La Concha bay), surf the beginner-friendly **Zarautz** beach (2.5 km of sand, 15–20 min away by bus) on your own each morning, and book **1–2 private coaching sessions** to level up. The surf base *is* the city, so there's no separate "city finish" to arrange. Evidence: [independent surf research](../research/surf-independent-and-city-surf.md).

## Itinerary

A 7-night, self-paced shape (book a shared Airbnb in central San Sebastián / Gros):

| Day | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| **Sat** | Fly NYC/SF → Bilbao (BIO) or San Sebastián (EAS) or Biarritz (BIQ). Bus/transfer to San Sebastián. Pick up rental boards + wetsuits (or arrange delivery). |
| **Sun** | Warm-up surf at Zarautz (mellow inside), explore Gros + Zurriola, evening pintxos crawl in Parte Vieja. |
| **Mon** | **Private/semi-private lesson** at Zarautz to reset fundamentals (pop-up, positioning). |
| **Tue–Wed** | Independent dawn/morning surfs at Zarautz; afternoons: La Concha, Monte Igueldo, Mt. Urgull, food. |
| **Thu** | **Second coaching session** (going down the line / wave selection), or a surf-explore day to Mundaka/Sopelana. |
| **Fri** | Free surf + day trip option: **Biarritz** (45 min, France) or **Bilbao** (Guggenheim, \~1h15). |
| **Sat** | Final morning surf, return boards; fly home (or 1 night Bilbao). |

## Cost Breakdown

Per person, mid-budget, shared Airbnb (2–4 people):

| Item | Estimate |
| --- | --- |
| Flights (NYC/SF, \~$700–1,100 RT) | $700–1,100 |
| Lodging 6–7 nts (shared Airbnb, $90–160/nt ÷ group) | $550–1,100 |
| Board + wetsuit rental, \~6 days | €70–110 (\~$80–120) |
| 2 private/semi-private lessons (\~€76 ea) | ~~€150 (~~$165) |
| Food & pintxos (it's a food city — budget up) | $250–450 |
| Local transport (buses to Zarautz, day trips) | $60–120 |
| **Total** | **≈ $1,600–2,700** |

Sources: [independent surf research](../research/surf-independent-and-city-surf.md) → [San Sebastián](../external-sources/surf-san-sebastian.md), [à-la-carte pricing](../external-sources/surf-independent-lessons-rentals.md), [flights](../external-sources/logistics-flights-august.md).

> [!NOTE]
> Lessons are a touch pricier than Portugal, but there's **no camp cost and no second city stop** — you're paying for one of Europe's best food cities as your everyday base. Pre-book the 1–2 lessons for August (summer demand). Atlantic water needs a wetsuit (provided). If the group wants *gentler/cheaper* waves, see [Ericeira/Lisbon](./surf-portugal-ericeira.md); for *warm water + lowest cost*, see [Costa Rica](./surf-costa-rica-nosara.md).
