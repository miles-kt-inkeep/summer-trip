## Overview

**Surf week in Nicaragua — San Juan del Sur (Playa Maderas) or Popoyo — the budget, uncrowded, all-day-offshore alternative to Costa Rica.** Nicaragua's signature edge is wind: sitting between Lake Nicaragua and the Pacific, it has **offshore winds 300+ days a year**, so waves stay clean much of the day instead of blowing out by midday. August is peak swell, water is boardshorts-warm (27–28 °C), crowds are a fraction of Costa Rica's, and costs are lower. The honest trade-off: a **US State Dept Level 3 "Reconsider Travel"** advisory and a connecting flight + longer transfer. Full comparison: [Nicaragua vs Costa Rica](../research/nicaragua-vs-costa-rica.md).

> [!WARNING]
> **Safety first — read before committing.** Nicaragua is at **Level 3 "Reconsider Travel"** (as of May 2026): crime, a stated **"serious risk of wrongful detention" of US nationals**, and limited healthcare. Costa Rica is the milder Level 2. This is the real deciding factor versus Costa Rica — the surf is better; the context is riskier. Check the [current advisory](../external-sources/nicaragua-surf.md) and your travel insurance before booking.

## Itinerary

A 7-night shape based in **San Juan del Sur** (gentlest/most social; Popoyo is the surf-first alternative):

| Day | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| **Sat** | Fly NYC/SF → **Managua (MGA)** via Miami / Houston / Panama (1 stop). Pre-arranged shuttle ~2.5–3 h to San Juan del Sur. |
| **Sun** | Settle in, board rental, warm-up surf; explore the SJDS bay + town. |
| **Mon–Fri** | **Surf Playa Maderas** (beginner/intermediate beach break, ~30–45 min shuttle/boat) mornings *and* afternoons thanks to all-day offshores; 1–2 coached/private sessions to level up. Optional day at Playa Hermosa or a Popoyo day trip ($30 shuttle). |
| **Sat** | Final surf; shuttle back toward Managua. |
| **Sun** | Fly home (or overnight near MGA for an early flight). |

*Popoyo variant:* base in Guasacate — learn at **Beginner Bay**, progress toward **Main Reef** ("basically always works"). Quieter, better waves, fewer beginners.

## Cost Breakdown

Per person, mid-budget. Generally **cheaper than Costa Rica** on the ground:

| Item | Estimate |
| --- | --- |
| Flights (NYC/SF → MGA, 1 stop) | $400–750 |
| Lodging 7 nts (hostel/guesthouse) or camp | $150–500 (camp bundle $750–$1,400) |
| Surf: board rental + 1–2 lessons | $120–250 |
| Food & drink (cheaper than CR) | $150–250 |
| Airport + SJDS/Popoyo shuttles | $80–160 |
| Travel insurance (recommended given advisory) | $40–100 |
| **Total** | **≈ $1,100–2,200** |

## Getting there (mid-Aug example)

> [!NOTE]
> No nonstop. **NYC →** JFK/EWR → Miami (AA) or Houston (United) → Managua, ~1.5–2.5 h layover. **SF →** SFO → Houston (United) or via Panama (Copa) → Managua. Then a **2.5–3 h pre-booked shuttle** to San Juan del Sur (or ~2.5 h to Popoyo). Build in a buffer; consider an overnight near MGA on the return.

## Bottom line

Nicaragua is the **better wave** (all-day offshores, fewer crowds, lower cost) but the **harder trip** (Level 3 advisory, connecting flights, longer transfer). Pick it if the group is surf-first and comfortable with the advisory; otherwise [Costa Rica](./surf-costa-rica-nosara.md) delivers similar August waves with far easier, safer logistics.
