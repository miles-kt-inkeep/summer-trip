## The one thing to understand first

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Surf "camps" come in **two shapes**, and the label hides the difference:
> - **All-inclusive bundle** — fixed daily-lesson schedule + meals (e.g. Lapoint, Dreamsea, Stoke). Simple, social, pricier; you follow the camp's rhythm.
> - **Accommodation + à-la-carte lessons** — a social surf-house base where you buy just the 1–2 sessions you want (e.g. Good People, Rapture, Zarautz Surf House). Cheaper, flexible — the best match for *"we can already surf, we just want a couple of coached sessions."*
>
> For your group (advancing beginners, ~5 lesson-days in), the à-la-carte camps — especially ones offering **video coaching / guiding** rather than just intro lessons — give the most improvement per dollar.

## Recommendation for this group

> [!TIP]
> - **Best overall fit → [Good People Surf, Zarautz](../trips/surf-san-sebastian-independent.md) (Spain).** À-la-carte so you're not locked into daily lessons, the **video-coaching + forecast-training** add-on is exactly what advancing beginners need, the vibe is social-not-party, and it pairs with San Sebastián. Summer-only — book early.
> - **Best value all-inclusive → Dreamsea, Costa Rica.** $445/wk dorm genuinely bundles 5 lessons + yoga + 2 meals/day — hard to beat if you want a fixed, warm, beginner-friendly week.
> - **Most polished/social bundle → [Lapoint](../trips/surf-portugal-ericeira.md) (Ericeira or Santa Teresa).** Pay more (~€600–900) for L1–4 + guiding, yoga, day trips, and a reliable 20s–30s crowd.
> - **If you mainly want to free-surf with a lesson or two → [Rapture Ericeira](../trips/surf-portugal-ericeira.md)** (lessons €40/day on demand) — see also the [independent surf options](./surf-independent-and-city-surf.md).
