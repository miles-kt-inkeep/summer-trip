## Question

Concrete mid-August flight examples for each trip gateway, from **NYC** and **SF**, **avoiding long/multi-stop layovers**. Worked window: **depart Sat Aug 15 → return Sat Aug 22, 2026** (any nearby week is similar; adjust ±days for fares).

## Sources cited

- [Flight routings by gateway](../external-sources/logistics-flight-routings.md)
- [Airfare price floors](../external-sources/logistics-flights-august.md)

## Findings

> [!TIP]
> **Rule of thumb:** from **NYC** nearly every gateway is a **nonstop**; from **SF** take **one** well-chosen connection (Newark for Lisbon, Madrid for Bilbao, Zurich for Geneva) and skip the 2-stop / 20h+ options the engines sometimes show. SF→Munich and SF→Liberia are nonstop.

### Surf options

| From | Gateway | Example routing (Sat Aug 15 out) | Stops / layover | Air time | Est. RT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **NYC** | Lisbon | TAP/United **EWR→LIS** overnight, arr ~morning Aug 16 | **Nonstop** | ~6h50 | $550–850 |
| **SF** | Lisbon | United **SFO→EWR→LIS** (connect Newark ~1.5–2.5h) | 1 stop | ~6h + 6h50 | $750–1,050 |
| **NYC** | San Sebastián (via Bilbao) | Iberia **JFK→MAD** (~7h) → **MAD→BIO** (~1h), layover ~1.5–2.5h | 1 stop | ~8h | $650–950 |
| **SF** | San Sebastián (via Bilbao) | Iberia **SFO→MAD** nonstop (~11h) → **MAD→BIO**, layover ~2–3h | 1 stop | ~12h | $850–1,150 |
| **NYC** | Liberia, CR | United **EWR→LIR** (dep ~07:18, arr ~11:44) | **Nonstop** | ~5h26 | $450–750 |
| **SF** | Liberia, CR | United **SFO→LIR** nonstop (~6h15); else 1 stop via IAH | **Nonstop**\* | ~6h15 | $550–850 |

\* Verify SFO→LIR frequency for the exact week; if not running, United via Houston (IAH) with ~1.5h layover.

### Meeting up (mixed NYC + SF group)

- Simplest: **everyone meets at the destination airport.** From SF, the single-connection routings above land within a couple hours of the NYC nonstops on the same day.
- For Lisbon specifically, SF travelers can route **through Newark** and join the NYC crew on the same TAP/United transatlantic leg.
