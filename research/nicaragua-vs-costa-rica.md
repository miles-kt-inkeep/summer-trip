## Question

## The one-line version

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Nicaragua wins on the surf itself; Costa Rica wins on everything around the surf.** Nicaragua has *all-day offshore winds* (300+ days/year), peak August swell, fewer crowds, and lower cost. Costa Rica has easier flights, more stability (Level 2 vs Nicaragua's **Level 3 "Reconsider Travel"**), better infrastructure, and the same warm water + consistent August waves. For a relaxed week where logistics and peace-of-mind matter, Costa Rica still edges it; for a surf-first group chasing the cleanest waves on a budget, Nicaragua is the better wave call — *if* you accept the advisory.

## Camps

Both countries split into **all-inclusive bundles** vs **hostel + à-la-carte lessons** (see [Costa Rica camp comparison](./surf-camp-comparison.md)). Nicaragua options ([src](../external-sources/nicaragua-surf-camps.md)):
- **SJDS / Maderas:** Rapture (local instructors), **360 Surf Camp** (5 lessons + meals + yoga bundle), Chica Brava (women's), SurfYogaBeer (video coaching).
- **Popoyo / Guasacate:** Popoyo Surf Lodge (all-inclusive, beginner package), Popoyo Surf Camp, 99 Surf Lodge; **Las Plumerias ~$750/wk all-in**, Waterways ~$1,399/wk.

## Which base, for this group

- **San Juan del Sur** — livelier backpacker/party town + the easy **Playa Maderas** beginner beach break. Best for a social, beginner-friendly week.
- **Popoyo (Guasacate)** — quieter, better/more varied waves; **Beginner Bay** for learning, **Main Reef** to progress. Best for surf-focused improvement.

## Recommendation

> [!TIP]
> - **Default → Costa Rica.** For a mixed group on a one-week trip, the nonstop flights, short transfer, and Level 2 stability outweigh Nicaragua's wind advantage. See the [Costa Rica proposal](../trips/surf-costa-rica-nosara.md).
> - **Choose Nicaragua if** the group is **surf-first**, budget-conscious, and comfortable with a **Level 3 advisory** + a connecting flight: you get cleaner all-day waves and fewer crowds. See the [Nicaragua proposal](../trips/surf-nicaragua.md). Base in **San Juan del Sur (Maderas)** for the gentlest, most social option.
> - **Want this warm/cheap Central American surf *without* the Level 3 advisory?** **El Salvador is now Level 1** — the direct safe upgrade. See [safe surf alternatives](./safe-surf-alternatives.md).
