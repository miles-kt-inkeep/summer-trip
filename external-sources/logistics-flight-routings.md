## Source

Carrier/routing facts per gateway (nonstop availability, flight times, best short-layover connections), aggregated from airline route pages and schedule aggregators (FlightsFrom, FlightConnections, SWISS, Lufthansa, Iberia, United, Delta, KAYAK), fetched 2026-06-02. Durations are scheduled block times; layover lengths are typical, not live-booked.

**Surf gateways**

- **Lisbon (LIS)** — *Ericeira / Lisbon.* **Nonstop from NYC:** TAP and United, EWR/JFK→LIS, **~6h45–7h05** (TAP ~19 nonstops/wk; United ~7/wk). **From SF:** no nonstop — 1 stop; cleanest via Newark (United/TAP) or a European hub.
- **Bilbao (BIO)** — *San Sebastián* (BIO ~1h15 by bus to San Sebastián). **No US nonstop.** **NYC:** 1 stop, best JFK→**Madrid** (Iberia, ~4/day, ~7h) → MAD→BIO (~1h). **SF:** 1 stop via **Madrid** (Iberia flies SFO–MAD nonstop ~11h) → BIO. Biarritz (BIQ, France, ~45 min to San Sebastián) is an alt, usually via Paris/Madrid.
- **Liberia, Costa Rica (LIR)** — *Nosara / Santa Teresa.* **Nonstop from NYC:** United EWR→LIR, **~5h26** (earliest dep ~07:18, arr ~11:44). **From SF:** United flies SFO→LIR **nonstop ~6h15** (started Dec 2021; verify seasonal frequency) — else 1 stop via Houston (IAH)/Denver. LIR→San José has no direct; 1 stop via IAH if needed.

**Hike gateways**

- **Venice (VCE)** — *Alta Via 1.* **Nonstop from NYC:** Delta JFK→VCE, **daily, ~8h35** (B767). **From SF:** no nonstop — 1 stop via FRA/MUC/ZRH/LHR/CDG/AMS (~13h50+ total).
- **Geneva (GVA)** — *Tour des Combins.* **Nonstop from NYC:** SWISS EWR/JFK→GVA, **~7h50** (year-round from JFK; ~6/wk summer 2026). **From SF:** 1 stop via **Zurich** (SWISS/United SFO→ZRH nonstop ~11h15) → ZRH→GVA (~1h).
- **Munich (MUC) / Innsbruck** — *Berliner Höhenweg.* **Nonstop from NYC:** Lufthansa/United, **~8h15**. **From SF:** Lufthansa/United SFO→MUC **nonstop ~11h05**. Then **Munich Airport → Innsbruck by train ~2h13**, frequent (~hourly), or train to Mayrhofen.

## Highlights

> NYC→Lisbon nonstop TAP/United ~6h45–7h05; NYC→Geneva nonstop SWISS ~7h50; NYC→Venice nonstop Delta ~8h35; NYC→Liberia nonstop United ~5h26.

> SF has *no* nonstop to Lisbon, Bilbao, Venice, or Geneva — but clean **single-stop** routings exist (Newark for LIS; Madrid for BIO; Zurich for GVA). SF→Munich and SF→Liberia are nonstop.
