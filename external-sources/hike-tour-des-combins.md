## Source

Verbatim-extracted detail from The Hiking Club's Tour des Combins planning page (fetched 2026-06-02).

- **Duration:** 7–11 days (typically 8–12 days)
- **Total distance:** 140 km (87 miles)
- **Elevation gain:** 8,450 m (27,750 ft)
- **Highest point:** Col des Otanes, 2,863 m (9,393 ft); 13 mountain passes total
- **Difficulty:** "Moderately challenging," suitable for experienced hikers. Significant elevation changes, some rocky/steep sections requiring confidence on uneven terrain. High-altitude exposure (2,500–2,900 m). Not technically demanding (no via ferrata noted); difficulty comes from ~1,000 m daily ascents/descents and long days.
- **Route:** Circumnavigates the Combins Massif around the Swiss–Italian border. 75+ accommodation options allow flexible daily routing. Alternate routes via Mont Rogneux, Lac de Louvie, etc.
- **Accommodation:** Mountain huts/refuges CHF 40–80/night (CHF 60–120 with meals); hotels/guesthouses CHF 100–200/night; campgrounds in valleys; limited wild camping above treeline.
- **Start/end options:** Bourg-Saint-Pierre, Le Châble, Mauvoisin (Switzerland), or Saint-Oyen (Italy). All 3–5 hours from Geneva or Milan airports.
- **Best season:** July through mid-September (longer daylight, low rain chance, no/low snow, warm temps, alpine flowers).
- **Estimated total cost:** Budget (camping/self-catering) CHF 500–1,000; mid-range CHF 1,200–2,000; comfortable CHF 2,000+. Hut dinners CHF 20–40, lunches CHF 10–20, transport CHF 20–50+.
- **Crowds:** "Relatively quiet," much less crowded than the Tour du Mont Blanc; some weekend day-hiker traffic near accessible huts.
- **Notable features:** Grand Saint-Bernard Pass (2,469 m), Corbassière Glacier (9.8 km), Corbassière footbridge (70 m above ground), Mauvoisin Dam (250 m).
