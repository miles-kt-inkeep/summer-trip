## Source

Verbatim-extracted detail from The Hiking Club's Alta Via 1 (Dolomites) planning page (fetched 2026-06-02). Captured as a strong third hiking candidate — same publisher as the Tour des Combins source, so detail is apples-to-apples.

- **Duration:** 6–10 days typically (shortenable to ~4, extendable)
- **Total distance:** 120 km (75 miles), classic route
- **Elevation gain:** 7,400 m (24,000 ft), south-to-north
- **Difficulty:** Moderately difficult. **"Unlike other Alta Via routes, this one does not require any via ferratas,"** making it more accessible — but it has "steep, rocky and exposed ridges." Long days (some hike 8+ h, others 4–5 h).
- **Accommodation:** 80+ rifugios/refuges, guest houses, hotels. **Bookings open in August the previous year and fill rapidly** — book early.
- **Start:** Lago di Braies / Pragser Wildsee. **End:** La Pissa bus stop (near Belluno, 20-min bus). Alt starts: Pederu, Lagazuoi/Cinque Torri, Passo Giau, Pescul/Alleghe.
- **Nearest big city:** **Venice** — primary arrival, ~4+ h to trailhead by bus/train. Innsbruck to the north; Belluno near the finish.
- **Best season:** Mid-June to mid-September; July–August peak (most crowded, accommodation books months ahead).
- **Estimated cost:** "€700 for a standard 7-night trip staying in dormitory accommodation," varies with room type/food/transport.
- **What makes it special:** Dolomites = UNESCO World Heritage "fairyland of the Alps"; alpine lakes, limestone pinnacles that change colour through the day, WWI history around Lagazuoi/Cinque Torri.
