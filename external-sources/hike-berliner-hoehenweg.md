## Source

Verbatim-extracted detail from Tyrol.com's Berliner Höhenweg (Zillertal Runde) page (fetched 2026-06-02), cross-checked with hiking-trails.com.

- **Days:** 7 stages (described as an 8-day circuit)
- **Total distance:** 71 km (hiking-trails.com cites ~85 km for the extended traverse)
- **Elevation gain:** ~5,300 m gain / ~5,000 m loss (hiking-trails.com: up to 6,600 m)
- **Difficulty:** Rated **Difficult — 5/6 fitness, 5/6 technique.** "High alpine, close to the glacier, steep and exposed." For experienced, skilled mountaineers; less experienced hikers should do partial stages or go guided.
- **Stages (hut-to-hut):**
  1. Gamshütte → Friesenberghaus
  2. Friesenberghaus → Olpererhütte
  3. Olpererhütte → Furtschaglhaus
  4. Furtschaglhaus → Berliner Hütte
  5. Berliner Hütte → Greizer Hütte
  6. Greizer Hütte → Kasseler Hütte
  7. Kasseler Hütte → Edelhütte
- **Start:** Brunnhaus 404, Finkenberg. **Finish:** Ahorn cable car station, Mayrhofen.
- **Best season:** Summer (June–September) when huts operate and snow permits.
- **Required equipment:** Crampons, grippy hiking boots, weatherproof layers, **harness for secured (cabled) sections**, GPS/map, provisions. Requires surefootedness and alpine experience.
- **Nearest cities:** Mayrhofen / Zillertal valley; Innsbruck airport ~1–1.5 h; Munich ~2 h.
