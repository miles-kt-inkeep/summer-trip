## Source

Aggregated from Surf Atlas San Sebastián guide, Devour Tours, and Surf Rental San Sebastián, fetched 2026-06-02. Captured because it's the ideal destination for the *independent + occasional lessons* model where the surf base is itself a major city.

- **San Sebastián (Donostia), Basque Country, Spain** — one of the world's great food cities (pintxos bars, La Concha bay, Michelin density) that is *also* a surf city. **Zona surf right in town** at Playa de la Zurriola (Gros). No separate "city finish" needed — the city *is* the trip.
- **Zarautz** — the learner beach: a 2.5 km sandy beach, "perfect for beginners," ~**15–20 min by bus / 12–15 min by car** from central San Sebastián. "Surfing conditions are almost always ideal here."
- **Lessons & rentals (à la carte):** single private lesson from **~€76** (off-peak); group-class packs (e.g. 7 classes ~€210); lessons include board + wetsuit. Board rentals (soft-top → shortboard) and wetsuits widely available; some companies deliver gear to your accommodation.
- **August conditions:** summer is the mellow/learner season on the Basque coast (smaller, manageable beach-break waves); autumn brings the bigger Atlantic swell. Water needs a wetsuit (provided).
- **Access:** fly to San Sebastián (EAS), Bilbao (BIO, ~1 h 15 by bus), or Biarritz (BIQ, France, ~45 min). Easy add-on: Biarritz/Bordeaux or a night in Bilbao (Guggenheim).
