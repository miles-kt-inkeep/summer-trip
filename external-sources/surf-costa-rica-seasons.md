## Source

Aggregated from Costa Rica surf-season guides (Rise Up Surf, Surf Atlas, LatAm Surfing, Gypsy Cab Nosara), fetched 2026-06-02.

- **August = rainy/"green" season on the Pacific coast, which is also peak surf season.** Consistent south swells from Southern-Hemisphere storms "roll in like clockwork" → bigger, more powerful, more consistent waves than the dry season.
- **Rain pattern:** "It may rain every day in rainy season, but it's often in the afternoon, leaving plenty of hours of sunshine or at least dry skies every morning." → surf mornings, relax/explore afternoons.
- **Skill-level reality:** Wet season "tends to cater more to higher-level intermediates to more advanced surfers as larger swells are more common." Beginners can still find forgiving inside sections at many spots.
- **Nosara / Playa Guiones:** Year-round waves, chilled vibe, versatile — "even when sets are overhead, there's usually a smaller peak working somewhere." Good for mixed-ability groups.
- **Santa Teresa:** Punchy beach breaks, livelier town feel.
- **Water temp:** ~27 °C — boardshorts, no wetsuit.

## Highlights

> "Intermediate and advanced surfers score long lefts and rights all along the Nicoya coast, while beginners can still find forgiving sections closer to shore."

> Rain "is often in the afternoon, leaving you plenty of hours of sunshine... every morning."

## My notes

Honest caveat for an *advancing-beginner* group: August's bigger south swells favor intermediates. Nosara/Guiones is the safer pick because its versatility means there's almost always a manageable peak; Santa Teresa is punchier. The group's ~5 days of prior lessons means a couple of guided/coached mornings would help them handle the step-up. Analysis in research/.
