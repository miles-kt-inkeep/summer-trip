## Source

Aggregated from Surf Atlas "Where to Surf in August" and corroborating August surf-destination guides (Surfer, MyWaveFinder, Rapture), fetched 2026-06-02. August = peak Northern-Hemisphere summer, so the question is where surf is both *good* and *learner-friendly*.

Warm-water, consistent August options that suit advancing beginners → intermediates:

- **Costa Rica (Pacific — Tamarindo, Nosara, Santa Teresa):** Air ~30 °C, water ~27 °C. Peak season on the Pacific coast; good for all levels thanks to consistency, warm water, friendly surf towns. (Rainy/"green" season — rain usually afternoons.)
- **Nicaragua (Pacific):** Consistent waves, water ~84 °F (29 °C), no wetsuit needed. Prime August window.
- **Peru (Máncora, Chicama):** Dry season, offshore winds, warm; Máncora a sandy-bottom left good for learners; Chicama a famous long left.
- **Sri Lanka (Arugam Bay):** Warm water, consistent season swell, mellow point — "long cruise" feel, good for improving fast.
- **Indonesia (Lombok — Gerupuk; Bali):** Solid S–SW swells; Gerupuk bay has 4 breaks for all levels; keep it mellow then step up. (Long-haul from US.)
- **Hawaii (Oahu — Waikiki):** August Waikiki is consistent, friendly crowds, slow mellow rollers — "perfect for learning or longboarding." North Shore is flat in summer.
- **Portugal (west coast — Arrifana, Amado; and Ericeira/Peniche region):** Summer waves are smaller, more manageable, consistent — ideal beginner→intermediate learning conditions.
