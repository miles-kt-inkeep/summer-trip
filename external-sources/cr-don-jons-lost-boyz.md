## Source

Don Jon's Lodge vs Lost Boyz — both surf accommodations in **Santa Teresa, Costa Rica** — from their own sites + Hostelworld/TripAdvisor, fetched 2026-06-02.

### Don Jon's Surf & Yoga Lodge
- **Location:** Santa Teresa, ~100 m from the beach; "the Santa Teresa beach break is only a few steps away"; southern tip of Nicoya.
- **Vibe:** "RELAX to the MAX" — chill surf + **yoga** lodge; mix of couples/families/backpackers; on-site **restaurant** (Latin American), free yoga. Long-standing ("Grupo Don Jon's").
- **Accommodation:** modern **apartments** (groups/families, AC), private **teak-wood cabins** (couples/small groups), and an AC **surf hostel** (backpackers). Orthopedic mattresses, fridges, daily cleaning, free coffee. Indicative private rooms/cabins **~$60–90/night** (hostel beds cheaper).
- **Surf:** camps **5+ days**; include accommodation, daily breakfast, **daily surf class + board**; optional yoga. **Private 1:1 lesson ~£50 (~$60).** Local certified instructors; all levels.

### Lost Boyz Hostel & Surf Camp
- **Location:** Santa Teresa, across from the beach; **3-min walk to sea, 5 min to the best breaks**; away from traffic.
- **Vibe:** social/party **backpacker community** — pool, treehouse, campfire, bar, snooker, free welcome shot, pool parties, daily activities. Created by "a multi-national group of long-term travelers and surfers"; community-focused, lively, budget.
- **Accommodation:** **dorms from ~$22/night** + private rooms; pool, lounges, restaurant & bar.
- **Surf packs (à-la-carte; lodging priced separately):** **1 week $250–650 = 2–5 lessons + 7-day board rental + t-shirt** (optional video analysis/photoshoot/quad). Lessons include **transport, rashguard, sunblock**. Airport transfer $120 extra. Many adventure add-ons (ATV, Isla Tortuga snorkel, bioluminescence, horse riding).

## Highlights

> Lost Boyz: "One Week $250–$650 (plus accommodation): 2–5 surf lessons, 7-day board rental..."; dorms from ~$22/night.

> Don Jon's: camps "5 days or more... include accommodation, daily breakfasts, daily surf classes and board"; private 1:1 ~£50.

## My notes

Both Santa Teresa, both steps from the beach, both all-levels — aimed at different travelers. **Don Jon's** = calmer, yoga, more private/comfort room options, **bundled** daily-lesson camp (better for couples/families or a chill week). **Lost Boyz** = social/party hostel, **flexible** surf packs + adventure add-ons, cheapest dorms (better for a young, social, budget group). Neither is confirmed Tico-owned (Lost Boyz = international traveler crew; Don Jon's ownership unstated). Comparison in research/.
