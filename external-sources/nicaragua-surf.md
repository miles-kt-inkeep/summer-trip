## Source

Nicaragua surf conditions, spots, logistics, and safety, aggregated from Giant's Foot Surf, Surf Atlas, Stormrider, Chica Brava, Rise Up, Ocean Fly Nicaragua, and the US State Dept, fetched 2026-06-02.

### Season & August
- **Best months = July & August** ("less rain, more sunshine and plenty of bigger waves"). Summer S/SW swell peaks Jul–Aug. **Water 27–28 °C** (boardshorts).
- Popoyo best **Apr–Oct**, when winds "drop almost dead" / stay offshore.

### The offshore-wind advantage (the key vs Costa Rica)
- Sitting between **Lake Nicaragua** and the Pacific, the Emerald Coast enjoys **offshore winds 300+ days/year** — the **Papagayo winds** funnel through the gap and deliver **all-day offshores** for much of Pacific Nicaragua. → clean, surfable waves much of the day, not just the dawn window.
- "Quality head-high+ reefs, points, and beach breaks with **a fraction of the crowds** down in nearby Costa Rica."

### Spots
- **San Juan del Sur (SJDS) region** — lively backpacker/surf town; "a wicked beginner surf destination." **Playa Maderas** = classic **beginner/intermediate beach break** (~30–45 min by boat/shuttle from town). Also Majagual, Hermosa, Remanso, Playa Gigante.
- **Popoyo / Tola region** (the surf hub, ~1 h N of SJDS): **Playa Guasacate** sand + reef, multiple peaks suit **beginner/lower-intermediate under ~7 ft**; **Beginner Bay** (south end of Guasacate) for complete beginners; **Main Reef** = high-quality A-frame that "basically always works," any tide, lightly offshore — **intermediate+**; **Outer Reef** = big-wave **advanced**; **Playa Colorado** (30 min S) = legendary **powerful beach break, intermediate/advanced**.

### Getting there & logistics
- **No US nonstop to the surf zone.** Fly to **Managua (MGA)** — the only intl airport; daily nonstops from **Miami (AA), Houston (United), Panama City (Copa)**. From NYC/SF = **1 stop** via those hubs.
- Managua → **San Juan del Sur ~2.5–3 h** drive; → **Popoyo/Las Salinas ~2.5 h**. SJDS↔Popoyo shuttle **$30/pp** (Tue/Fri) or private **$90–120**.

### Safety (honest, material)
- **US State Dept: Level 3 "Reconsider Travel"** (as of May 2026) — crime, **"serious risk of wrongful detention" of US nationals**, limited healthcare, arbitrary enforcement of local laws. (Costa Rica sits at the milder Level 2.)
- Beaches: strong currents; drownings have occurred — respect conditions.

## My notes

Nicaragua's surf case is genuinely strong — all-day offshores, peak August swell, fewer crowds, lower cost. The counterweight is real: a **Level 3 advisory** + harder logistics (no nonstop, 2.5–3 h transfer) vs Costa Rica's Level 2 stability and easier access. For an advancing-beginner group the right bases are **Playa Maderas (SJDS)** or **Guasacate/Beginner Bay (Popoyo)**. Analysis in research/.
