## Source

Aggregated from Ericeira surf-camp listings (BookSurfCamps, Lapoint, Rapture, Ericeira Surf Camp, AdventureInYou), fetched 2026-06-02.

- **Ericeira, Portugal** — Europe's only World Surfing Reserve; a dedicated surf town ~40 min north of Lisbon, between Sintra and Peniche. Cobblestone streets, seafood, surf shops, bars.
- **August conditions:** Summer brings generally smaller, more manageable, consistent waves — well suited to beginners and improvers. (Bigger Atlantic swell returns in autumn.) Many beach breaks plus reef/point options for when you progress.
- **Camps cater to all levels** (Lapoint runs levels 1–4 plus guiding; Rapture, Ericeira Surf Camp similar). Typical week package = shared/private accommodation, 2 lessons/day or coaching, board + wetsuit, breakfast, sometimes dinners, transfers.
- **Indicative pricing:** week-long surf-camp packages commonly land around **€500–800/person** in shared rooms (varies by operator, room type, season); private rooms higher. A 4-day intensive beginner/intermediate coaching package is offered by Hostel & Surfcamp 55, with 7-day options.
- **Skill fit:** Beginner-and-intermediate intensive coaching (2 surf lessons/day + theory) is explicitly offered — a good match for advancing beginners wanting to level up.
