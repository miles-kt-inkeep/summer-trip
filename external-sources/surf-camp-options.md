## Source

Per-camp offerings and pricing for named surf camps in Portugal, Spain, and Costa Rica, aggregated from camp pages and review sites (Surf Atlas, Dreamsea, Lapoint, Rapture, BookSurfCamps, SurfCampsEurope), fetched 2026-06-02. **Key distinction captured: "all-inclusive bundle" vs "accommodation + à-la-carte lessons."**

### Portugal — Ericeira (40 min N of Lisbon)

- **Lapoint Ericeira** — Package **~€600–900/wk** (Portugal camps typically €500–1,200). Includes daily surf lessons **levels 1–4 + guiding**, breakfast buffet, yoga, day trips (Sintra, vineyards), pool/garden. Lunch/dinner not bundled. Polished, social, 20s–30s. *Bundle.*
- **Rapture Ericeira (Lizandro)** — Flexible dates (no fixed weekly slots). Includes **breakfast + dinner Mon–Fri**; **surf lessons €40/day, paid separately** (gear incl.). Rooms dorm → private apartment. *Accommodation + à-la-carte lessons.*
- **Ericeira Surf Camp & Hostel** — **Customizable** packages by level (beginner/intermediate/advanced) + activities (yoga, bike, SUP, massage); build-your-own. In-town hostel vibe. *Flexible.*
- **Baleal Surf Camp (Peniche/Baleal alt)** — Oldest Portuguese surf camp (est. 1993), beachfront, family-run. *Bundle.*

### Spain — Zarautz / San Sebastián (Basque)

- **Good People Surf (Zarautz)** — Glamping (bell/cabin tents), **summer only**. From **~€195/wk accommodation + breakfast**; optional **intensive surf package = 5 guided surfs/wk + 2 video-coaching sessions + forecast training**. Eco, "best-vibes," Spanish BBQ, live music, free gear use. *Accommodation + à-la-carte lessons.* Strong for improvers (video coaching).
- **San Sebastián Surf Camp × Stoke Surf House** — Converted Basque farmhouse, 20 min from town. **8-day package = intro lesson + daily guided surfs**; open bar (€15/eve); **party-focused**, self-paced. *Bundle (party).*
- **Zarautz Surf House** — Budget backpacker hostel, 75 m from beach, on-site café; **lessons purchased separately** (ISA instructors, max 6/group). *Accommodation + à-la-carte lessons.*
- **North Shore Surf Camp** — In-town, **family-friendly**, junior programs (11–14, 15–18), year-round. *Bundle.*
- **Pukas Surf Eskola** — Established Basque surf school (San Sebastián/Zarautz); **lessons/courses only**, not lodging-bundled. *Lessons only.*

### Costa Rica

- **Lapoint Santa Teresa** — Package **from €745/pp**. Includes surf + breakfast; **lunch & flights not included**. Polished, social (same brand as Ericeira). *Bundle.*
- **Dreamsea (Tamarindo)** — **$445 dorm → $1,500 river suite per week**; all packages = **5 surf lessons/wk + daily yoga + breakfast & dinner** (VAT extra). Glamping/jungle vibe, beginner-focused. *Bundle.*
- **Safari Surf (Nosara)** — Reputable, sustainability-certified surf school/camp in Nosara; all-inclusive surf-and-stay packages (price on request, typically premium). *Bundle.*

## My notes

The split that matters for this group: **bundles** (Lapoint, Dreamsea, Stoke, North Shore) hand you a fixed daily-lesson schedule; **à-la-carte** camps (Good People, Rapture, Zarautz Surf House) give you a social base + cheap lodging and let you buy just the 1–2 sessions you want — exactly the "not a full camp" model. Good People's *video coaching + forecast training* add-on is the most improver-friendly. Comparison + recommendation in research/.
