## Source

Aggregated à-la-carte (non-camp) surf pricing for the independent "rent a board + book a lesson or two" model, fetched 2026-06-02. Covers Ericeira/Lisbon (Portugal) and Santa Teresa/Nosara (Costa Rica).

**Ericeira / Lisbon, Portugal**
- Board rental: ~**€15/day** soft-top (beginner), ~**€20/day** shortboard — wetsuit included. Multi-day/weekly rates lower per day.
- Drop-in lesson: ~**€40/person** (board + wetsuit included); private lessons higher. Lessons bookable per-session via surf schools or GetYourGuide.
- Schools: Surf Riders & Co, Ericeira Surf House, plus many GetYourGuide listings. Costa da Caparica (20 min from central Lisbon) lets you base in Lisbon itself and still surf with lessons.

**Santa Teresa / Nosara, Costa Rica**
- Board rental: **$15–25/day** (Nalu, Surf Nosara, Dreamsea, Ollie's).
- Private lesson: **~$50–70 for 2 hours**, certified instructors, hotel pickup often included.

Implication: a week of independent surfing + 2 lessons costs roughly **€80 + €70–110 board rental** (Portugal) or **$100–140 lessons + $90–150 board** (Costa Rica) — i.e. **~$200–300 of surf services**, vs. €500–800 for a full camp.
