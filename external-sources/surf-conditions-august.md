## Source

August surf-condition data per spot, aggregated from Surf Atlas, surf-forecast.com, Surfline, Dreamsea, and Witch's Rock, fetched 2026-06-02.

### Ericeira, Portugal
- **Waves:** Small in summer — "waist-high on a good day, knee-high on a quiet one, genuinely flat on occasional dead spells." Gentle, forgiving whitewater — ideal for beginners.
- **Water temp:** Peaks **20–22 °C (68–72 °F)** ~Aug 20 (warmest of the year). **Caveat:** when the **Nortada** north wind blows hard, upwelling can drop it from 18 °C to **15 °C overnight.** Wetsuit needed.
- **Wind:** **Nortada blows onshore from ~midday**, turning clean morning waves to chop → **surf mornings.** Beginner beach **Foz do Lizandro** (sand-bottom) is protected from north wind.
- **Crowds:** Busy in summer.

### Zarautz / San Sebastián, Spain
- **Waves:** Small, **under 1.5 m**, calm — "good for beginners, not experienced surfers seeking larger swells." Summer is the small/inconsistent season (can go flat).
- **Water temp:** Peaks **20–25 °C (68–77 °F)** ~Aug 10 — warmest month. Springsuit typical.
- **Air/weather:** Max ~23 °C, min ~15 °C; **cool, cloudy, foggy; rainfall constant** (~42 rain days / 162 mm across the season — the Basque coast is green/wet).
- **Crowds:** Beaches quite crowded in summer.

### Nosara & Santa Teresa, Costa Rica (Pacific)
- **Waves:** **August = peak season**, consistent S/SSW swells. **Nosara** avg **2–3 ft, sets 3–4 ft+**; **Santa Teresa** avg **2–3 ft, sets 3–5 ft** (punchier). Beginners find forgiving inside sections; bigger sets favor intermediates.
- **Mornings:** **Clean/glassy, light offshore** (NE/NNE <5–10 mph) — **dawn patrol ideal**; afternoon onshore + rain.
- **Water temp:** **29–30 °C** — boardshorts, no wetsuit.

### Tamarindo, Costa Rica (Dreamsea base)
- **Waves:** Aug = "mini-summer," consistent SW swells + offshore, manageable crowds. Beach break typically **3–5 ft**, mellow at high tide with **long whitewater rides**; also small 30–60 cm sections. "One of the best learn-to-surf breaks in Costa Rica" — more sheltered/forgiving than Santa Teresa.
- **Water temp:** **25–30 °C (77–86 °F)** — warm, no wetsuit.

## Highlights

> Ericeira August: "waist-high on a good day... ideal for beginners," water 20–22 °C but Nortada can crash it to 15 °C; surf mornings before the onshore wind.

> Zarautz August: waves "not exceeding one and a half meters," water 20–25 °C — "a good season for beginners rather than experienced surfers."

> Costa Rica August: peak season, Nosara 2–3 ft (sets 3–4 ft+), Santa Teresa sets to 5 ft, glassy offshore mornings, water 29–30 °C.

## My notes

The decisive contrasts for an advancing-beginner group: **consistency** (Costa Rica peak/reliable; Spain riskiest for flat days), **forgiveness** (Ericeira Foz + Tamarindo gentlest; Santa Teresa punchiest), and **water** (CR boardshorts; Portugal coldest with Nortada risk). All three regions reward **morning** sessions. Analysis in research/.
