## Source

Nicaragua surf camps + lodges, aggregated from Surf Atlas, BookSurfCamps, 360-surf, Popoyo Surf Lodge, Chica Brava, Las Plumerias, fetched 2026-06-02. Same **bundle vs à-la-carte** split as Costa Rica.

### San Juan del Sur / Playa Maderas
- **Rapture Surfcamp Maderas** — on the hills above Playa Maderas; **local instructors**; beginner lessons Mon–Sat, progressing as you advance. *Bundle.*
- **360 Surf Camp SJDS** — **bundle:** accommodation, daily breakfast + 7 dinners, **5 surf lessons (10 h, max 4/instructor)**, 3 yoga, board/equipment hire, transfers, Sunday Playa Hermosa excursion.
- **Chica Brava** — **women's** camp; expert female coaching, accommodation, meals, yoga, massage. *Premium.*
- **SurfYogaBeer** — beginner-friendly; daily lessons, 1:1 coaching, **video review**, chef meals (fixed-date trips).

### Popoyo / Guasacate
- **Popoyo Surf Lodge (PSL)** — "Nicaragua's first all-inclusive surf camp"; **7-night beginner package** from the basics. *Premium.*
- **Popoyo Surf Camp** — beachfront, all levels; instructors focused on progression.
- **99 Surf Lodge** — **6-day beginner camps**; lively/upscale.
- **Las Plumerias Lodge** — **all-inclusive from ~$750/wk.**
- **Waterways** — packages **from ~$1,399/wk.**
- **Surf Ranch Popoyo**, **Magnific Rock**, **Big Surf House** — range of mid/budget options.

### Price read
- A **budget hostel + à-la-carte lessons in SJDS** is cheapest (generally below Costa Rica). All-inclusive lodges run **~$750 (Las Plumerias) → ~$1,400 (Waterways)** and up to premium (PSL, Chica Brava).

## Highlights

> 360 Surf Camp SJDS: "5 surf lessons (10 hours)... maximum 4 students per instructor," plus yoga, meals, transfers.

> Las Plumerias all-inclusive "from $750 per week"; Waterways "from $1399 per week."

## My notes

For an advancing-beginner group: **Rapture or 360 (SJDS)** for beginner-friendly bundles near the easy Maderas beach break, or **Popoyo Surf Lodge / Popoyo Surf Camp (Guasacate)** for progression with better/varied waves. SJDS = livelier/party + easier beginner beaches; Popoyo = better waves + quieter. Comparison in research/.
